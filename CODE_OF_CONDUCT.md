# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the Contributor Covenant. In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming.
- Assume good intent; disagree constructively.
- No harassment, discrimination, or trolling.
- Respect privacy and do not share private information.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open a confidential issue or contact the maintainers privately. Persistent violators may be removed from participation.
